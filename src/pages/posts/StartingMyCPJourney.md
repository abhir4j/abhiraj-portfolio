I have been interested in programming for a long time. I mostly enjoy making websites & discord bots, trying new ideas, nd building things with code.

Recently, I decided to start competitive programming too.

I am still new to it. I cannot solve hard problems yet, and Ion know all the algorithms or data structures. But I want to learn.

At first, competitive programming looked difficult to me. There are many problems, strange logic, nd people solving them very fast. But I realised that everyone starts somewhere.

So, I am starting from the basics.

I want to solve problems regularly and improve slowly. Some days I may solve only one easy problem. Some days I may not solve anything. That is okay. I just want to keep learning nd not give up quickly.

I think competitive programming will help me think better. It will teach me how to stay calm when a problem looks confusin nd how to find a soln step by step.

This blog will be where I share wot I learn. I will write about problems I find interesting, mistakes I make, nd small things that help me understand better.

This is my first post nd the start of something new for me.

Let’s see how far I can go.
