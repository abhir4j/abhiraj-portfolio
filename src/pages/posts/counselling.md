These past few days have been confusing.

My WBJEE counselling is going on. In the first round, I got Metallurgical Engineering at Jadavpur University. I was happy because getting a seat there felt like a big thing.

But during physical verification, I could not provide my EWS certificate. Because of that, my seat was cancelled and my category was changed from EWS to General.

Honestly, it was disappointing.

For a moment, I thought I had things figured out. Then suddenly, I was back to thinking about everything again.

Now, round 2 choice filling is going on. I am mainly confused between TMSL CSE with AI/ML and Heritage ECE.

Both feel different.

CSE with AI/ML is closer to the coding side of things, which I enjoy. Heritage ECE is also a good option, but it would take me more into electronics. I have been thinking about both a lot.

The round 2 result will come on 21 July.

Right now, I do not know what will happen. I do not know which college or branch I will finally get. But I am trying not to panic.

Maybe not everything goes according to plan. Maybe sometimes you lose one option and have to look at other paths more carefully.

This is just where I am right now: waiting, thinking, and hoping for the best.

Let’s see what happens on 21 July.
